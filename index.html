Privacy Policy

How the iOS game handles information and advertising choices

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use Brick Blast: Neon Breaker (the “App”), an iOS game. The App provides gameplay features, optional advertising rewards, and Apple Game Center functionality.

1. Gameplay and information stored on your device

The App stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as your progress, saved games, settings, achievements-related state, and Daily Challenge results. This information is used to provide and restore gameplay features. Locally stored information generally remains on the device until you clear it or uninstall the App, subject to iOS backup and restoration behavior.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are shown only when you choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers (where permitted), ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, performance information, and diagnostic data. These data may be used to deliver and measure advertising, help prevent fraud, and improve advertising services. The data collected and how it is used may vary based on your location, privacy preferences, operating system settings, and Google's practices.

For information about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent and advertising privacy choices

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices regarding advertising and related data processing. You may be able to accept, decline, or manage applicable purposes and vendors. Where required, you can revisit these choices using the App's Privacy / Manage Ad Privacy settings.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements from the App. You can also manage applicable device-level advertising and tracking preferences through iOS settings.

4. Apple Game Center

If you use Apple Game Center, Apple may process information associated with your Game Center account and gaming activity, such as your Game Center display name, leaderboard scores, and achievements. Game Center services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and the privacy settings for your Apple account.

5. Sharing and service providers

The App uses third-party services including Google AdMob and Apple Game Center. Information may be processed by those providers as described above and in their respective privacy notices. The developer does not operate a separate account-registration system for this App.

6. Data retention, deletion, and your rights

Gameplay information stored locally may be removed by deleting the App, subject to any applicable device backups or system restoration features. Google and Apple manage data processed through their respective services according to their own policies and retention practices.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal information and to withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis. To ask a privacy question or request assistance concerning information handled by the App's developer, contact the address below. For data held directly by Google or Apple, you may also use their privacy controls and contact channels.

7. Children's privacy

The App does not offer a separate child account service. Advertising and data-handling requirements for children may differ depending on age and region. If you believe information from a child has been handled inappropriately, please contact the developer using the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, or applicable requirements. The current version will be posted at this page with an updated effective date.
